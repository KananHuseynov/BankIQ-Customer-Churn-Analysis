# 🏦 BankIQ — Bank Customer Churn Analysis

## About the Project
Analyzed 10,000 bank customer records to identify customers at risk of churning using SQL, Python, and Tableau.

## Tools Used
- **SQL Server** — data querying and analysis
- **Python** (pandas, matplotlib, seaborn) — data cleaning and visualization
- **Tableau Public** — interactive dashboard

## Key Findings
- 🇩🇪 German customers churn at **2x the rate** of other countries (32%)
- 👴 Customers aged **51-60** have the highest churn rate (56%)
- 🚨 **99%** of customers who filed a complaint left the bank
- 😴 Inactive members churn at **2x the rate** of active members (27% vs 14%)
- ⚠️ Customers with 3-4 products have **83-100%** churn rate

## Business Recommendations
1. Launch a targeted retention program for German customers
2. Immediately address complaints — they are the strongest churn signal
3. Re-engage inactive members through personalized campaigns
4. Develop special offers for the 51-60 age group

## File Structure
